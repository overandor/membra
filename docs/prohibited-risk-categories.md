# MEMBRA Prohibited Risk Categories

## Risk Doctrine

MEMBRA starts with low-risk, easy-proof household utility.

MEMBRA avoids high-liability categories until trust, insurance, verification, and compliance rails mature.

## Prohibited Categories (Phase 0)

### High Liability
- Vehicles (cars, motorcycles, boats, aircraft)
- Firearms and weapons
- Hazardous materials
- Medical devices and equipment
- Prescription medications

### High Privacy Risk
- Smart home devices with cameras
- Baby monitors
- Security cameras
- Home automation systems with remote access

### High Regulatory Risk
- Food and beverage storage (fridge space for consumables)
- Alcohol storage
- Tobacco products
- Controlled substances

### High Access Risk
- Entire home access
- Bedroom access
- Bathroom access
- Home office access with confidential documents

### High Proof Difficulty
- Services requiring subjective quality assessment
- Custom fabrication or manufacturing
- Professional services (legal, medical, financial)
- Creative work with intangible deliverables

## Restricted Categories (Phase 1 - Require Additional Verification)

### Medium Liability
- Power tools (require deposit, proof of training)
- Ladders (require deposit, insurance)
- Lawn equipment (require deposit, insurance)
- Heavy appliances (require deposit, insurance)

### Medium Privacy Risk
- Guest room access (require identity verification)
- Living room access (require identity verification)
- Shared workspace access (require identity verification)

### Medium Regulatory Risk
- Pet boarding (require licenses, insurance)
- Plant care (require species verification)
- Specialized storage (climate control requirements)

## Allowed Categories (Phase 0 MVP)

### Low Risk Storage
- Shelf space
- Closet space
- Garage space
- Storage bins
- Under-bed storage

### Low Risk Tools
- Hand tools
- Small power tools (drills, sanders)
- Cleaning equipment (vacuums, mops)
- Lighting equipment (ring lights, lamps)
- Charging stations

### Low Risk Access
- Seating (chairs, stools)
- Tables and desks
- Workspace areas
- Charging/workspace access

### Low Risk Fulfillment
- Package pickup/delivery
- Handoff services
- Local errands
- Inspection services

## Category Approval Criteria

A category is approved for MEMBRA when it meets:
1. **Low Liability**: Minimal risk of damage, theft, or injury
2. **Easy Proof**: Clear before/after verification possible
3. **Clear MUP**: Minimum Useful Price calculable
4. **High Repeatability**: Potential for repeat transactions
5. **Low Fulfillment Friction**: Simple handoff or access
