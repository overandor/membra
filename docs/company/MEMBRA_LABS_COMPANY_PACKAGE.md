# MEMBRA Labs Company Package

## Company

**MEMBRA Labs** builds proof infrastructure for physical-world monetization.

The company converts real-world assets, physical media, QR/NFC activity, proof events, and payout eligibility into verified commercial records.

## Flagship Product

**MEMBRA Proof Network** is a proof-backed physical media and local asset monetization system.

It enables a controlled workflow:

1. Owner registers a physical surface, object, location, wearable, or local asset.
2. Advertiser or sponsor funds a campaign or placement offer.
3. MEMBRA generates a QR/NFC media kit or proof task.
4. Owner confirms receipt or installation.
5. Owner submits proof evidence.
6. Admin or automated review checks the evidence.
7. Scan/tap events route through MEMBRA-controlled attribution links.
8. Payout eligibility is unlocked only after proof rules pass.
9. Reports are generated for owners, advertisers, operators, and investors.

## First Commercial Wedge

The first wedge is **verified micro-out-of-home physical media**.

Examples:

- QR stickers on cars, shop windows, bags, flyers, and signs
- NFC tags on physical media kits
- campaign shirts, hats, patches, badges, and delivery bags
- event proof tasks
- local business scan/tap attribution
- proof-reviewed owner payout eligibility

This wedge is narrower, clearer, and more sellable than the full long-term household inventory vision.

## Long-Term Vision

MEMBRA becomes the physical-world proof and settlement-control layer for local inventory, asset access, household utility, routes, proof events, and verified human commerce.

Long-term tagline:

> Every verified home, object, surface, person, route, and local asset can become a priced, permissioned, risk-scored economic node.
