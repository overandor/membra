# MEMBRA Labs Resale Appraisal

## Scope

This appraisal is for the repository/IP portfolio only. It is not a formal business valuation, tax appraisal, securities analysis, legal opinion, or M&A fairness opinion.

## Current Classification

MEMBRA Labs is currently best classified as:

> early-stage prototype/IP company package.

It is not yet proven as:

> operating revenue company.

## Evidence Supporting Value

- coherent company thesis
- flagship product frame: MEMBRA Proof Network
- main repo with FastAPI + Gradio runtime
- ads/proof FastAPI scaffold
- wallet relay FastAPI scaffold
- React/Vite QR dashboard package
- module-level repo separation
- documentation, appraisal, risk, and claim artifacts

## Current Gaps

- no verified customer revenue
- no production auth system across suite
- no unified production database
- no live proof-review operation
- no signed pilots in repo evidence
- no legal/compliance review package
- no payment/payout production proof
- no buyer-ready demo video or data room yet

| Scenario | Estimated Range |

## Current Appraisal

As currently packaged after company consolidation docs:

**$40,000–$100,000 fair-market prototype/IP value**

Potential strategic value to a buyer who wants the thesis, naming, modular architecture, and early codebase:

**$75,000–$150,000**

Replacement-cost plus strategic optionality may exceed this, but cash resale value should not be overstated without usage, revenue, or pilots.

## Appraisal Positioning

Use this wording:

> MEMBRA Labs is an early-stage prototype/IP company for proof-backed physical-world monetization. It includes runnable scaffolds, a coherent product system, and a modular repository portfolio. It is not yet represented as a revenue-generating operating company.

Avoid this wording until proven:

> MEMBRA is an acquisition-ready operating company with revenue.

## Sale Strategy

Best buyer types:

- local adtech startup
- QR/NFC attribution company
- proof-of-work marketing agency
- print-on-demand/vendor automation company
- crypto wallet experimenter seeking non-custodial relay UX
- hackathon/IP buyer
- accelerator or venture studio

- IP assignment
- repo transfer
- limited handoff support
- optional advisory retainer
- no unsupported claims of revenue or guaranteed performance
