## 2026-04-28 13:28 America/New_York — Couchify baseline

Couchify was identified as being at the `prototype → product shell` stage. The original product thesis was a public/private workflow-browsing platform where exported ChatGPT or Markdown history could become a browsable, gated, monetizable knowledge archive.

Initial components included:

- public workflow reader;
- premium implementation vault;
- Google OAuth / Clerk identity gate;
- sensitive-data redaction and obfuscation layer;
- founder workflow voyeurism model;
- node/session/assets priced by the minute;
- dark orange / black neomorphic and glassmorphic interface direction.

Original MVP path:

```text
chat-export.md
→ parsed timeline
→ redacted public version
→ premium implementation vault
→ Stripe unlock
→ deployed Vercel app
```

## 2026-04-28 13:31 America/New_York — AI Witness Node expansion

Couchify expanded from a workflow archive into a physical shared-space system using a laptop or camera as a consent-based AI witness. The room could document activity, generate summaries, and provide post-session artifacts while preserving privacy through visible, blurred, muted, or excluded modes.

Key concept:

> Couchify AI Witness Node is a consent-based room system where a host places a laptop or camera in a shared space, participants choose privacy mode, and the system produces AI-generated session artifacts.

## 2026-05-09 — ReInventory / NeighborTools / NeedNearby expansion

The product expanded from spaces into local inventory. The thesis became that people already own useful things from Amazon and other retailers, and those goods can be re-inventoried locally.

Key modules:

```text
ReInventory       → local redistribution of items people already own
NeighborShelf     → user-approved apartment inventory
NeighborTools     → rent tools from people nearby
NeedNearby        → demand-first request stream
Couchify Requests → request layer integrated with spaces and tools
```

Core line:

> Why wait for shipping when your neighbor already has it?

## 2026-05-09 — SKU-as-a-Service / Inventory-as-a-Service

MEMBRA was recalibrated around SKU-as-a-Service and Inventory-as-a-Service.

Core definitions:

- **SKU-as-a-Service:** every approved item, space, skill, action, or service becomes a local sellable/rentable SKU.
- **Inventory-as-a-Service:** apartment-owned goods become searchable, local, permissioned inventory.
- **Host-as-a-Service:** residents become apartment operators.
- **Rent-as-a-Service:** users rent nearby inventory through LLM chat.
- **Belongings Assessment-as-a-Service:** AI assesses what a resident can earn from.
- **AdAgent-as-a-Service:** the LLM promotes approved inventory to nearby demand.

Central slogan:

> Your apartment is the nearest warehouse.
